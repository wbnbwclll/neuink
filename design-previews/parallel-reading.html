Towards Faithful Visual Explanations for Multimodal Models

Y. Chen · M. Park · R. Singh · CHI 2026

3. Measuring Explanation Faithfulness

Visual explanations are often evaluated by how convincing they appear to people, but plausibility alone does not guarantee that highlighted evidence influenced the model's prediction.

We distinguish explanation plausibility from explanation faithfulness. The former reflects human expectations; the latter asks whether the explanation represents the model's internal decision process.

3.2 Cross-modal perturbation

Our evaluation removes visual and textual evidence independently. A faithful explanation should predict the resulting output change across both modalities.

A. Lopez · N. Kumar · UIST 2025

4. User Study

Participants preferred explanations that combined concise textual rationales with localized visual evidence. Confidence increased when both channels agreed.

Perceived usefulness was driven more strongly by clarity and actionability than by measured faithfulness. This gap complicates the design of explanation interfaces.

4.3 Design implications
